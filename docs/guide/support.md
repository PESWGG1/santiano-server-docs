## 赞助说明

感谢你对 Santiano 服务器的支持。本服务器为非商业服务器，不以营利为目的，所有赞助均遵循以下原则：

### 资金用途

- 全部赞助仅用于服务器运行产生的直接成本，例如主机、带宽、域名、备份存储与基础运维。
- 赞助结余将保留用于后续运营，不用于任何个人获利或商业投资。
- 在适当时机，我们会在官网公示阶段性收支概况。

### 赞助性质

- 赞助为自愿、无偿、不可退还的捐赠行为，不构成商品或服务购买。
- 我们不会通过任何方式向玩家销售游戏内物品、货币、数值或排他性权限。
- 赞助金额与游戏体验无关，服务器内不会出现付费变强或付费优先的内容。

### 唯一回馈

- 赞助者仅会获得“灯塔 / 星火”荣誉称号，用于标识其对服务器运营的支持。
- 该称号不提供任何游戏内数值、物品、命令、权限、排队或管理方面的优势。
- 除上述称号外，不提供其他任何游戏内好处或商业化回报。

### 声明

- 本服务器不承接商业广告，不以赞助名义提供品牌曝光或推广服务。
- 如对赞助用途有疑问，可通过服务器官方渠道联系管理组查询。



## Sponsorship Information

Thank you for supporting the Santiano server. This server is operated as a community project and is not intended to generate profit. All sponsorships follow the principles below.

### Use of Funds

- All contributions are used exclusively to cover direct operating costs, including hosting, bandwidth, domain registration, backup storage, and essential maintenance.
- Any remaining funds will be reserved for future operating expenses and will not be used for personal profit or commercial investment.
- When appropriate, periodic summaries of income and expenses will be published on the official website.

### Nature of Sponsorship

- Sponsorships are voluntary, non-refundable donations and do not constitute the purchase of goods or services.
- We do not sell in-game items, currencies, statistical advantages, or exclusive privileges.
- Contribution amounts do not affect the gameplay experience. The server offers no pay-to-win or paid-priority benefits.

### Sole Recognition

- Sponsors may only receive the honorary **“Beacon / Spark”** title in recognition of their support for the server’s operation.
- This title provides no advantages involving in-game statistics, items, commands, permissions, queues, or administration.
- No other in-game benefits or commercial rewards are provided.

### Declaration

- The server does not accept commercial advertising or provide brand exposure or promotional services in exchange for sponsorship.
- Questions regarding the use of sponsorship funds may be directed to the administration team through the server’s official channels.
